There are 84,302 games on Steam. I scored every one of them. And the most underrated game on the entire platform has 1,914 reviews. Ninety-eight percent of them positive.
For context, that's fewer reviews than a Counter-Strike skin has trades in an hour.
Here's how I found it. I took every game with at least 500 reviews. That's 19,000 games. Then I asked one question. Given how good this game is, how many people should have played it?
Price matters. Genre matters. Whether it came out the same week as Elden Ring really matters. Ask Tunic.
The model predicts the review count. The gap between that prediction and reality is the number I care about. Big gap, good game, nobody showed up.
Number ten is a farming game. I know. Stay with me.
It launched in March 2021, sold to about 40,000 people, and has a 96 percent rating. The model thinks it should have sold 900,000. Why didn't it? It came out four days after Valheim.
